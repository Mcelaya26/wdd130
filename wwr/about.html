Wild Current Rafting Co.

To provide safe, empowering, and unforgettable river experiences while fostering respect for wild waterways, teamwork, and personal growth. We believe time on the river builds confidence, strengthens connection, and teaches people to move with nature rather than against it.

History

Wild Current Rafting Co. began in 2014 after founder Caleb Rivers noticed a growing gap in the rafting industry: plenty of thrill-focused outfitters, but very few that truly supported beginners and families who wanted to learn the river rather than just survive it. Drawing on his experience as a guide, Caleb set out to create trips that emphasized safety, confidence, and a welcoming introduction to whitewater.

Starting with two rafts, a borrowed trailer, and weekends only, Caleb ran trips himself while Maya helped design safety training and Evan volunteered to manage logistics. Word spread quickly about the company’s patient guides and safety-first approach. Within three seasons, Wild Current expanded into a full-time operation with a dedicated training program and a loyal base of returning guests.
